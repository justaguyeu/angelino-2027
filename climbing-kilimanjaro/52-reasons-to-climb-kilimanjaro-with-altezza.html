What is a B Corp?
Certified B Corps are for-profit companies independently vetted by the non-profit B Lab. They must meet rigorous standards in:
  • Social & environmental performance
  • Operational transparency
  • Accountability to all stakeholders (not just shareholders)
To earn the certification, a company must score 80+ on the B Impact Assessment, make a legal commitment to stakeholder governance, and publish its full impact profile publicly.
Why it matters:
  • As a certified B Corp, Angelino is legally required to consider the impact of its decisions on people, communities, and the environment — not just profit. We’re committed to ethical tourism, local empowerment, and environmental responsibility. We do not partner with companies that promote hunting, animal exploitation, or other ethically questionable activities. Our compliance and impact are verified through independent audits every three years.
Results and support:
  • For you, this means verified standards you can trust, travel that supports local jobs and communities, and a company committed to long-term responsibility.
  • Supporters of the B Corp include Leonardo DiCaprio, Angelina Jolie, former U.S. Presidents Al Gore and Bill Clinton, Nobel Prize–winning economist Robert Shiller, and business leaders like Richard Branson of Virgin and Paul Polman of Unilever.

Safety
30. A comprehensive approach to high-altitude safety
The Kilimanjaro climbing industry remains poorly regulated, and not all operators follow proper safety standards. Some expeditions are led by unqualified guides and lack clear medical protocols for detecting and preventing altitude sickness. Combined with overly short itineraries that do not allow for safe acclimatization, this often leads to avoidable emergencies on the mountain. At Angelino, we do things differently. Our strict safety protocols cover every aspect of the expedition — from equipment inspections and medical kit preparation to emergency communication and evacuation procedures. Each of our mountain guides is fully trained and equipped to recognize, prevent, and treat the early signs of altitude sickness, ensuring your safety on every step of Kilimanjaro.
31. Medical check-ups every morning and evening
At Angelino, every climber has a personal medical record for the entire expedition. Twice a day, our guides conduct health checks, measuring vitals such as oxygen saturation, pulse, and blood pressure, and recording the results, as well as your subjective well-being, to track your condition at altitude. This system allows us to detect the earliest signs of altitude sickness and respond immediately, ensuring your safety at every stage of the climb.

We run the Angelino Academy — a training program for the next generation of Tanzanian tourism professionals.
Through workshops, courses, and hands-on practice, we equip local youth with skills that empower them to build careers in the tourism sector. At the Academy, we train guides, drivers, and porters, helping them learn a new career or master new skills, including first aid, high-altitude medicine, navigation, leadership, and emergency response.

Climb Preparation
We take your safety very seriously
Before each expedition, our paramedic Philip Swart and the local medical team brief all guides using your health and dietary data. They review every climber’s medical notes, risks, and dietary needs — so your safety is carefully planned, not left to improvisation.

You’re Never Alone with Angelino
Organizational Structure
We do everything possible to ensure your comfort and safety in the mountains, and we’re confident that no one does it better. But life can be unpredictable, and what truly matters is knowing that whatever happens, we’ll always be there to help. If your insurance company refuses to immediately authorize a helicopter evacuation in a life-threatening situation, we’ll cover the cost ourselves first and then help you handle the paperwork with them later. If you fall ill and can’t fly home right away, we’ll offer you a complimentary stay at one of our hotels whenever we have available rooms. With Angelino, you’ll never be left to face a difficult situation on your own, not even after your expedition is over. People trust us because we care for them like family. There are no fine-print conditions or empty promises, only genuine responsibility and care for every person who travels with us.
