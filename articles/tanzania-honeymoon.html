Is Tanzania good for honeymoon? The ultimate Tanzania honeymoon guide.

20662
Reading time: 8 min.
About Tanzania

Tanzania’s stunning landscapes, incredible wildlife, and rich culture make it an unforgettable honeymoon destination, offering the perfect mix of adventure and relaxation. Couples can enjoy climbing Kilimanjaro at sunrise, floating over the Serengeti in a romantic hot air balloon, or simply relaxing on Zanzibar’s beautiful beaches. With so many unique experiences, Tanzania is ideal for creating lasting memories.

This guide explores the top romantic spots and activities in Tanzania, helping couples find just the right balance of adventure and tranquility for their dream honeymoon.

The peak tourist season in Tanzania is from January to early March and from late June to September, with sunny coastal weather and ideal dry conditions for safaris. However, if you’re looking for a more private experience away from the crowds and at better rates, consider planning your honeymoon during the off-season. While you may experience brief rain showers, you’ll find fewer tourists and the national parks will be lush and vibrant.
Tanzania is the ideal honeymoon destination.  Image source: Shutterstock, by Nikita Burdenkov

Climbing Kilimanjaro together

Kilimanjaro is the highest peak in Africa. The mountain’s gentle slopes make reaching Uhuru Peak possible even without a mountaineering experience. Good physical fitness is all you need. If you both enjoy active vacations, visit Tanzania to conquer Kilimanjaro, an experience you’ll treasure for a lifetime.

Our other guide provides all the details you’ll need for a successful ascent to the Roof of Africa.

During the ascent, you’ll trek for approximately 4–6 hours each day, with support from guides, porters, and cooks. Each camp will provide you with breakfast, lunch, and dinner. The team carries a first-aid kit and oxygen tanks to prepare for unforeseen situations. The total duration of the tour varies based on the chosen route, but the climb typically takes around 6 to 8 days.

After witnessing a breathtaking sunrise on the Roof of Africa in a truly romantic setting, you can either embark on a safari in Tanzania or unwind on the beautiful beaches of Zanzibar.

Blogger Jonny Melon with his girlfriend during their ascent of Kilimanjaro. Image source: Angelino Adventures archives
When is the best time to climb Kilimanjaro? Kilimanjaro tours run year-round. From late December to early March, the weather around the mountain is warm and dry, making it an ideal time to plan an ascent. However, keep in mind that many other travelers know this too, so the trails and camps can be crowded. From mid-March to the end of May, the spring tropical rains begin, and snow may fall at the summit. The upside is that there are far fewer tourists, providing a more private experience. The rains stop in June, marking the start of the second dry season, which lasts until late October. November and December bring rain again, but despite this, demand traditionally peaks around the New Year holidays.

Is a safari worth it for a honeymoon in Tanzania?

If you prefer active adventures to beach lounging, a Tanzania safari tour could be perfect for your romantic getaway. Whether you pick the Northern or Southern Safari Circuit, you’ll get to explore the expansive African savanna, spot wild animals and exotic birds, visit ancient volcanic craters, and even swim by stunning waterfalls. With so much to offer, let’s explore some of the must-see spots for an unforgettable Tanzania honeymoon.

Arusha National Park

Arusha National Park offers a unique experience of observing wildlife on foot, rather than from a vehicle. Naturally, this is done from a safe distance and under the close supervision of an experienced ranger. Arusha National Park boasts tropical forests, volcanoes, and waterfalls. It is also home to Mount Meru, Tanzania’s second-highest volcano after Kilimanjaro.

Tanzania’s waterfalls are a popular spot for couples in love. Image source: Angelino Adventures archives

The area around the alkaline Momella Lakes has a unique romantic ambiance. Large flocks of pink flamingos gather here, creating a breathtaking sight. You can paddle a two-person canoe while enjoying the sounds of birdsong. This experience lasts 2–3 hours and leaves you with cherished memories for years to come.

Lake Manyara National Park

Lake Manyara National Park is another perfect spot to watch flamingos. Often referred to as the "pink lake," Manyara attracts thousands of these beautiful birds. As you stroll along its shores, you might also encounter the park's unique tree-climbing lions, true "royal couples" of the animal kingdom. These majestic felines rest in the grand fig trees during the day, descending at sunset to search prey. This combination of breathtaking scenery and wildlife encounters makes Lake Manyara National Park an ideal destination for honeymooners seeking to create memories together.

Serengeti National Park

Covering 14,763 km² (5,700 mi²), the Serengeti is one of the most famous destinations in East Africa, known for its rich diversity of African wildlife and landscapes. Here, you can encounter the Big Five: elephants, lions, buffalos, rhinos, and leopards. The legendary Great Migration of wildebeest flows through the valleys of the Serengeti, where you can also spot zebras, giraffes, hippos, and many other wildlife species. Whether you opt for the Northern, Central, or Southern Serengeti, each spot has its own unique take on the great migration and offers something special to experience. For birders, Serengeti National Park features over 500 different bird species. It's one of Africa's premier safari destinations and a perfect spot for a honeymoon in Tanzania, especially for those who appreciate exotic nature and seek unforgettable adventures.

The Serengeti is the third-largest national park in Tanzania, and to truly appreciate its beauty, you may consider adding an extra day to your visit. Dedicated nature lovers can rent a game lodge or stay at one of the hotels or tented camps located within the park.
A honeymoon safari will create unforgettable memories for a lifetime. Image source: Angelino Adventures archives
Drones are not allowed in the Serengeti, but you can enjoy a hot air balloon ride to take in the breathtaking views of the savanna and capture photographs. This romantic experience can become a memorable highlight of your honeymoon.

Tarangire National Park

The Tarangire River flows through the Tarangire National Park and serves as a vital source of fresh water for the region. Here, you can witness a remarkable scene: majestic elephants gather in large herds, digging into the riverbed to access groundwater. Other savanna inhabitants, drawn to the area by thirst, observe this process keenly.

Cheetahs in Tarangire National Park.  Image source: Angelino Adventures archives

The park boasts stunning acacia forests and towering century-old baobabs. It’s also a haven for bird enthusiasts, with over 550 bird species calling Tarangire home. Like the Serengeti, you can enjoy a hot air balloon ride here, perfect for capturing photos and videos of your Tanzanian honeymoon.

Ngorongoro Crater

According to UNESCO, the Ngorongoro Conservation Area has around 25,000 large wild animals. The main draw for visitors is the Ngorongoro Crater. This area was once the site of a volcano, and today it features a vast caldera that provides ideal conditions for wildlife. As a result, it has the highest concentration of wild animals in all of Tanzania.

Zebras in the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.  Image source: Angelino Adventures archives

A significant feature of the area is its population of critically endangered black rhinos, with only several thousand individuals remaining worldwide.

When is the best time to go on safari? The rainy and dry seasons in Tanzania's parks usually coincide. If you're looking for sunny, dry weather, consider planning your safari between January and February or from July to October. Remember that these months are part of the high tourist season, so parks can be crowded, and prices may be elevated. Though rain may be more frequent at other times of the year, it transforms the dry savanna into lush greenery, creating stunning landscapes.

Is Zanzibar nice for a honeymoon?

Zanzibar is an ideal beach honeymoon destination within Tanzania. With its lush palm groves, pristine white sandy beaches, and the azure waters of the Indian Ocean, it offers breathtaking scenery and a captivating romantic ambiance. And with just a short, 1.5-hour flight from Kilimanjaro International Airport, paradise is closer than you think.

Scenic views of the Zanzibar waterfront.  Image source: Angelino Adventures archives

The northern part of Zanzibar is more accommodating for tourists, as the beaches here experience minimal tides. You'll find many hotels and boutique accommodations, including private villas right by the Indian Ocean. Notable options include Zuri Zanzibar, Essque Zalu, and Xanadu Luxury Villas Zanzibar.

However, the island part of Tanzania is not just for relaxing on the beach. Zanzibar also provides a variety of activities to enrich your honeymoon experience.

Stone Town. As you wander through the cobblestone streets of Zanzibar's capital, you'll come across many ancient architectural landmarks. The Old Dispensary showcases intricately carved wooden decorations in Indian style, while the Palace Museum was once the sultan's residence. Be sure to visit the famous Old Fort and its beautiful Forodhani gardens. You can also check out the Freddie Mercury Museum, honoring one of Zanzibar's most notable natives. Be sure to admire the beautiful St. Joseph’s Cathedral, explore Darajani market, and browse the souvenir shops lining the streets. 

The Old Fort Tower in Stone Town.  Image source: Shutterstock, by Olha Solodenko

One great option for accommodation in Stone Town is Park Hyatt Zanzibar. This five-star hotel is situated along the coast, with part of it housed in a 17th-century mansion and the other in a modern building. Other options include Tembo B&B Apartments in the Shangani area and Zanzibar Coffee House, which dates back to 1885.

Kitesurfing and scuba diving. For an active honeymoon, Paje Beach is an excellent choice, especially for those who love kitesurfing. The area is consistently windy, and the water remains shallow near the shore. You'll find the best waves from June to October and from December to March.

On the southeast coast of Zanzibar, you'll find Jambiani Beach, which is ideal for snorkeling. This area is vibrant and lively, featuring numerous restaurants, bars, and cafes along the shore and a variety of nearby hotels to choose from.

Diving is a great way to diversify your beach experience in Zanzibar. Photo source: Angelino Adventures archives

Spice Plantations. Expansive spice plantations cover large areas of the archipelago. Knowledgeable local guides will share fascinating insights into the history and cultivation techniques of the aromatic plants. During your tour, you’ll stroll through gardens brimming with flowering nutmeg, pepper, clove, cinnamon, vanilla, and coriander. You'll also get the chance to sample a variety of exotic fruits, including papaya, durian, jackfruit, and more.

Jozani Natural Forest Reserve. Jozani Forest, situated in central Zanzibar, is the habitat of the critically endangered red colobus monkey, a rare member of the primate family. These agile monkeys are curious and sociable, often interacting freely with tourists and rarely hiding behind the dense foliage. They love to pose for cameras, offering fantastic opportunities for wildlife photography.

Jozani Forest is home to many red colobus monkeys. Image source: Shutterstock, by Guillaume Angleraud

Secluded Relaxation on Pemba Island. Pemba, one of the most picturesque and least populated islands in the Zanzibar archipelago, is a paradise for loving couples. With its white sandy beaches, turquoise waters, and minimal tourist traffic, Pemba provides an intimate setting for a romantic getaway. If you want to spend your honeymoon in privacy and away from the crowds, Pemba is the perfect choice. Here, you can enjoy tranquility without any disturbances.

The island's population is primarily centered in the small town of Chaki Chaki, where residents still wear traditional clothing and converse in Swahili. Visiting Pemba offers a wonderful opportunity to immerse yourself in the authentic atmosphere of island Africa.

You can reach Pemba Island by flying with a local airline. The flight from the main island takes just half an hour. We recommend staying at The Aiyana Resort & Spa, which Angelino Adventures has partnered with for many years. This boutique resort features 30 well-equipped villas nestled in beautiful surroundings. The professional staff offers top-notch service, and guests rave about the wonderful restaurant and spa center.

When is the Best Time to Go to Zanzibar? The tourist seasons in Zanzibar align with those on the Tanzanian mainland, featuring dry and sunny weather from January to February and again from July to September. The rainiest months, which are not ideal for beach vacations, are April to May and November to December. For the rest of the year, any rain tends to be brief. The average daytime temperature hovers around 28°C (82°F).
