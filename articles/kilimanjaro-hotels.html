Kilimanjaro hotels: top 10 places to rest before the climb

10098
Reading time: 8 min.
Climbing

Mount Kilimanjaro, in northeastern Tanzania, is surrounded by scenic accommodations. Most are located in Moshi, the main base for trekkers visiting Kilimanjaro National Park. The city offers a wide range of modern, comfortable, and safe hotels, perfect for relaxing before your climb or recovering afterward.

Since 2014, Angelino Adventures has organized Kilimanjaro tours, including hotel stays, for thousands of climbers. Based on that experience, we’ve selected the 10 best hotels near Mount Kilimanjaro for your stay:

  • Brubru Lodge — a small, 10-room hotel in a quiet district of Moshi.
  • Aishi Machame Hotel — set right at the foot of Kilimanjaro, just a 10-minute drive from the Machame route trailhead.
  • Chanya Lodge — a cozy lodge with a beautiful garden, only 6 km (3.7 mi) from downtown Moshi.
  • Kilimanjaro Coffee House — a hillside hotel near Kilimanjaro’s arabica coffee plantations.
  • Kaliwa Loge — situated at 1,300 meters (4,265 ft) above sea level, surrounded by Kilimanjaro’s lush tropical forest.
  • Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel — a four-story hotel offering modern comforts in the heart of Moshi.
  • Maridadi Hotel — a Moshi-based hotel with architecture inspired by traditional local design.
  • Parkview Inn — a centrally located hotel with secure grounds and a restaurant featuring vegetarian options.
  • Kilemakyaro Mountain Lodge — a mountainside resort perched at 1,134 meters (3,720 ft), offering scenic views and tranquility.
  • Salinero Kilimanjaro Hotel — a complex of private cottages in a quiet, green part of Moshi.

1. Brubru Lodge 3*

The hotel is located in the quiet and scenic Shanty Town district of Moshi, offering rooms with stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Just a 10-minute drive away, you’ll find local markets and souvenir shops. About an hour from the property lies a traditional Chagga village, which you can visit on a tour. If you're visiting in November, you’ll see the jacaranda trees in full bloom, covering the area in vibrant purple blossoms.

Brubru Lodge offers a garden setting with a 24-hour open swimming pool, along with two restaurants, one of which is an open-air venue. The rooms are designed for a comfortable stay, featuring modern, tasteful interiors, quality furnishings, cozy beds, and reliable hot showers.

Brubru Lodge in the shade of sprawling palm trees. Image source: Angelino Adventures archive

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 50 minutes.

Location: Moshi city.

Accommodation: 10 double rooms with one double bed or two single beds.

Services: free Wi-Fi, luggage storage, laundry service, airport transfer, complimentary bottled water.

Hotel website: brubru-lodge.com

2. Aishi Machame Hotel 3*

If you're wondering where to stay to see Kilimanjaro views, the cozy Aishi Machame Hotel is a great option. Located just a 10-minute drive from the Machame route trailhead, it’s especially convenient for those planning a climb.

The hotel is surrounded by a lush, flowering garden that’s home to charming local wildlife like small blue monkeys and squirrels. On the grounds, you’ll also find peaceful outdoor lounge areas, a bar, a restaurant, and a swimming pool.

Aishi Machame Hotel and its blooming garden. Image source: Aishi Machame Hotel website

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 40 minutes.

Location: Machame region, Kilimanjaro.

Accommodation: 29 double and single rooms. One triple room with three single beds.

Services: free Wi-Fi, souvenir shop, laundry service, airport transfer, luggage storage, complimentary bottled water.

Hotel website: aishi-machame.com

The hotel was truly amazing and located in a peaceful place and helped to calm down our mind prior to the expedition. Food was awesome and service exceptional. Date of stay: September 2024.

3. Chanya Lodge 3*

Just 6 km (3.7 mi) from the lively center of Moshi, Chanya Lodge offers a peaceful retreat, far enough from the city bustle, yet close enough. The hotel is about an hour’s drive from Kilimanjaro Airport (JRO). Surrounded by a garden, the property features a spacious outdoor pool and a full-service spa.

Family-run since 1988, Chanya Lodge has been welcoming guests for decades, offering warm hospitality and everything you need for a comfortable stay.

Swimming pool in the enclosed relaxation area of Chanya Lodge. Image source: Tripadvisor. Photo by Matt

Location: Moshi city.

Accommodation: 32 semi-bungalow-style rooms — single, double, and triple.

Services: free Wi-Fi in the hotel restaurant, massage and other wellness treatments, yoga and spa, safe and luggage storage, parking, souvenir shop, and equipment rental point: gear for Kilimanjaro climbs and camping safaris.

Hotel website: chanyalodge.co.tz

They make their own local coffee which taste amazing. If you are thinking of walking the Kilimanjaro this is a perfect place to relax afterwards :-) Date of stay: October 2024.

4. Kilimanjaro Coffee Lodge 3*

The eco-friendly Kilimanjaro Coffee Lodge sits on the slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro, next to thriving Arabica coffee plantations, at an elevation of 1,550 meters (5,085 feet). The hotel is owned by a company that specializes in growing and producing high-quality arabica coffee.

Inside the main building, you’ll find six cozy double rooms that offer sweeping views of the surrounding coffee fields. The lodge also includes a lounge area, an open-air restaurant, and a spa center. In the inner courtyard, there’s a swimming pool nestled in a lush tropical garden. 

Stunning view from the hotel grounds on the slopes of Kilimanjaro. Image source: Kilimanjaro Coffee Lodge

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 1.5 hours.

Location: about 30 minutes by car from Moshi city.

Accommodation: 6 spacious double rooms.

Services: bar, fireplace lounge, spa center, outdoor relaxation area.

5. Kaliwa Loge 3*

Nestled at the foot of Mount Kilimanjaro, Kaliwa Lodge is surrounded by the vibrant greenery of tropical mountain forest. Perched at 1,300 meters (4,265 ft), the lodge offers breathtaking views of the mountain, best enjoyed from its spacious terrace. The resort features ten minimalist-style bungalows that are both comfortable and thoughtfully designed to harmonize with the natural landscape.

Just minutes from the Machame route trailhead, the lodge is ideally located for trekkers heading up the mountain. Guests can also enjoy an on-site bar and a restaurant serving a mix of European, Asian, and African dishes.

View from the hotel terrace. Image source: Tripadvisor, photo by KaliwaLodge

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 50 minutes.

Location: Machame region, Kilimanjaro.

Accommodation: 10 rooms, including single, double rooms with twin beds, and triple options.

Services: free Wi-Fi, laundry service, airport transfer (both ways), luggage storage, free parking, souvenir shop, massage.

6. Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel 3*

Unlike the few Kilimanjaro hotels perched on its slopes, Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel is located in the heart of Moshi, surrounded by modern city life. Its central location makes it easy to explore the town before your trek, whether you're browsing local markets, picking up souvenirs, or getting a feel for the region's culture and daily rhythm.

The hotel’s four-story building includes a fitness center, and the rooftop hosts an open-air bar. Outside, guests can enjoy a swimming pool and a dedicated play area for children.

Facade of Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel in downtown Moshi. Image source: Tripadvisor. Photo by Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 50 minutes.

Location: Moshi city.

Accommodation: 44 rooms, including grand suites of 50 m² (538 sq ft), suites of 34 m² (366 sq ft), and standard rooms with double beds of 23 m² (248 sq ft).

Services: free Wi-Fi, safe, luggage storage, airport transfer.

7. Maridadi Hotel 3*

Maridadi Hotel is a boutique property in Moshi that showcases authentic African style. Its distinctive sand-colored exterior sets it apart from many other resorts in the area. Inside, the modern and comfortable rooms are decorated in calming, earthy tones, creating a restful atmosphere.

The hotel grounds feature a garden-framed swimming pool, which often serves as a stage for live entertainment, ranging from music performances to acrobatic shows.

Original architectural design of Maridadi Hotel. Image source: Maridadi Hotel website

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 50 minutes.

Location: Moshi city.

Accommodation: 24 standard rooms, single and double. Some rooms have two single beds.

Services: free Wi-Fi, massage, complimentary welcome drink, barbecue area and equipment.

8. Parkview Inn 3*

Most Kilimanjaro hotels are located in Moshi, and Parkview Inn is a standout among them. This modern hotel offers all the essentials for a comfortable stay. One of its highlights is a poolside patio with views of Mount Kilimanjaro — an ideal setting if you're hoping to enjoy the mountain without leaving town.

Guests often mention the spacious, air-conditioned rooms with thoughtful layouts, as well as the on-site restaurant, which serves a variety of local and Indian dishes, including options for vegetarians.

Poolside patio in the enclosed area of the hotel. Image source: Parkview Inn website

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 50 minutes.

Location: Moshi city.

Accommodation: 50 rooms, including Deluxe and Junior Suites.

Services: free Wi-Fi, 24-hour security patrol, safe, luggage storage, foreign currency exchange.

9. Kilemakyaro Mountain Lodge 3*

Located on the slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro in a quiet rural setting, Kilemakyaro Mountain Lodge sits at an altitude of 1,134 meters (3,720 ft) and offers a peaceful retreat away from the bustle of Moshi. It’s a great choice for those planning to summit Uhuru Peak, the highest point on Kilimanjaro, or anyone who simply enjoys mountain trekking. The drive from Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) takes approximately 1.5 hours.

Kilemakyaro Mountain Lodge is the perfect place if you’re looking for where to stay to see Kilimanjaro. The spacious rooms feature king-size beds, providing the perfect space to unwind and recharge after a challenging high-altitude climb.

Outdoor swimming pool under palm trees on the slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro. Image source: Tripadvisor, photo by MyDustyDesk

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 1h30m.

Location: Uru Kifumbu, Moshi region.

Accommodation: 40 spacious soundproof rooms — single, double, and triple.

Services: Wi-Fi, free parking, laundry service, and airport transfer (both ways).

10. Salinero Kilimanjaro Hotel 3*

Rounding out our list of Kilimanjaro hotels is Salinero Kilimanjaro Hotel, a tranquil resort made up of private cottages, tucked away in a quiet, green corner of Moshi. Despite its peaceful setting, it’s just 3 kilometers (1.9 miles) from the city center and about 45 km (28 miles) from Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), making it easily accessible in under an hour by car.

The hotel features a spa center, conference and banquet facilities, a restaurant and bar, as well as separate swimming pools for adults and children. Guests stay in charming, standalone cottages equipped with all the essentials for a comfortable stay, including a TV, a kettle, a hairdryer, and a mini fridge.

Nearest airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), 50 minutes.

Location: Moshi city.

Accommodation: 30 rooms, including honeymoon and non-smoking options.

Services: free Wi-Fi, parking, outdoor dining area, massage, barbecue area, dry cleaning, complimentary welcome drink, luggage storage, souvenir shop.
