Best hotels in Moshi: your guide to a perfect stay

Best hotels in Moshi: your guide to a perfect stay

9371
Reading time: 9 min.
About Tanzania

Moshi Town is the gateway for travelers visiting Tanzania to climb Kilimanjaro, whether it's their first stop before the ascent or their last after the journey. Nestled at the foot of Kilimanjaro, Moshi has been a welcoming hub for travelers for many years.

Where you stay can turn Moshi Town into more than just a stopover, offering a chance to unwind, immerse yourself in African culture, and soak in the local atmosphere.

To help you find the perfect stay, the Angelino Adventures team has compiled a list of the best Moshi hotels and hostels known for their comfort, authenticity, and warm hospitality.

Brubru Lodge

Brubru Lodge is a new, modern hotel in Moshi. Image source: brubru-lodge.com

This 10-room boutique hotel opened in 2023 and has become a cozy retreat for climbers and safari lovers. The project's creators drew inspiration from the diverse world of birds inhabiting northern Tanzania. You can even spot some of them in the hotel's lush green garden, which features a pool and cozy relaxation areas.

The hotel grounds have everything you need for a comfortable stay

The restaurant serves a continental menu featuring high-quality, fresh ingredients from local farms. Led by a European-trained chef, the kitchen offers a variety of dishes for meat lovers, vegetarians, vegans, and those on a gluten-free diet. Guests can specify their dietary preferences or allergies when booking, and the hotel will ensure that the chefs tailor meals accordingly.

Brubru Lodge’s restaurant

Other advantages of the hotel include the fastest Wi-Fi in the region, a 24-hour front desk, free luggage storage for guests, and the option for a comfortable airport transfer to and from Kilimanjaro Airport for an additional fee.

Website: brubru-lodge.com

Aishi Machame Hotel

Aishi Machame allows travelers to enjoy modern comforts surrounded by Tanzania’s natural beauty

The hotel is just 30 minutes from Kilimanjaro Airport and 15 minutes from the starting point of the Machame Route, the most popular trail on Kilimanjaro. The hotel is surrounded by a thriving natural habitat, home to monkeys, bush babies, and other gentle African wildlife. This tranquil setting makes Aishi Machame an ideal retreat for climbers and travelers seeking peace and comfort away from the city's hustle and bustle.

Nearby, guests can explore a lively village and a tropical forest or take a guided tour to the stunning Materuni Waterfall. The experience includes visits to fruit and coffee plantations, offering a chance to meet and connect with the local indigenous community.

Travelers can unwind by the pool under the Tanzanian sun before or after their climb

The on-site restaurant boasts a two-level terrace where guests can savor continental and Mediterranean cuisine, as well as authentic Swahili dishes. The bar serves everything from freshly brewed Tanzanian coffee to an extensive selection of wines and fresh juices.

Aishi Machame provides all the essentials for a relaxing stay before and after climbing Kilimanjaro

Guests can choose from single, double, and triple rooms and enjoy amenities like a spacious pool with sun loungers, high-speed Wi-Fi, luggage storage, and a laundry service. Airport and city transfers are also available for an additional fee.

Website: aishi-machame.com

Kaliwa Lodge

On clear days, guests can enjoy an impressive view of Mount Kilimanjaro from the spacious shared terrace. Image source: kaliwalodge.com

Perched at an altitude of 1,300 meters (4,265 feet), Kaliwa Lodge is another climbers’ favorite. It’s conveniently located just 30 minutes from Kilimanjaro International Airport, near the Machame Gate, one of the main entry points to the National Park.  

The cozy bungalows are nestled within a dense tropical mountain forest. The spacious terrace offers breathtaking views of the Roof of Africa and the lush Weru Weru River valley below. The lodge’s restaurant serves fusion cuisine, while its whisky bar remains open until midnight, adding to the hotel’s appeal.

Website: kaliwalodge.com

Pink Flamingo Boutique Hotel

Pink Flamingo Boutique Hotel is a great choice for a getaway with friends. Image source: pinkflamingotanzania.com

The family-run Pink Flamingo Boutique Hotel is also considered one of the best in Moshi. It is surrounded by lush greenery and features three separate relaxation areas, including a large communal pool and two private pools.  

The hotel features a spa offering various beauty treatments and relaxing massages, including specialized options for guests preparing to climb Kilimanjaro or recovering afterward.

The hotel features multiple pools. Image source: pinkflamingotanzania.com

The restaurant specializes in Italian cuisine and serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner at set times. Guests who plan to dine outside these hours are requested to inform the staff a few hours in advance.  

For accommodation, guests can choose from a range of room categories in the main boutique hotel or opt for private villas. In both cases, the management ensures attention to detail and professional service.

Website: pinkflamingotanzania.com

Kilimanjaro Coffee Lodge

The villas at Coffee Lodge come with terraces that offer serene views of the lush green garden. Image source: macharecoffee.com

The eco-hotel Kilimanjaro Coffee Lodge sits on a coffee estate on the mountain's southern slope, about 90 minutes from the airport and 30 minutes from downtown Moshi. The hotel is located in the Mweka area, home to the Mweka Route, which is used exclusively for descending Kilimanjaro. This is an important detail to remember when booking: if you plan to climb Kilimanjaro, getting to the starting point may not be as convenient.

For those who visit Moshi to relax and enjoy nature, Coffee Lodge is a fantastic place to stay. Guests can choose from six up-to-date double rooms with views of the coffee fields, while the main house offers an open-air restaurant and bar with stunning panoramic views of the snow-capped Roof of Africa.

One of the hotel's standout features is its natural pool. Image source: macharecoffee.com

Guests also have access to a cozy lounge, a spacious pool, and a spa area.

Website: macharecoffee.com

Chanya Lodge

The hotel management promises all guests a warm, home-like atmosphere and genuine hospitality. Image source: chanyalodge.co.tz/

Chanya Lodge is conveniently located on the outskirts of Moshi, an hour from the airport and just 15 minutes from the bustling town center. Its prime location offers easy access to the National Park gates, making it ideal for climbers and travelers looking to explore Tanzania’s natural beauty and rich culture.  

This hotel is one of the oldest in the region. Established in 1988, it has remained family-run, with the business passed down through generations. Chanya Lodge offers 32 guest rooms, an open-air restaurant and bar serving Tanzanian, Indian, and continental cuisine, a pool, and a spa for ultimate relaxation.

The hotel's swimming pool is set amidst lush tropical greenery. Image source: chanyalodge.co.tz

Exceptional service is at the heart of the hotel's philosophy. The warm and welcoming staff aim to make you feel like part of their Tanzanian family, ensuring Chanya Lodge feels like a home away from home.

Website: chanyalodge.co.tz

Ameg Lodge Kilimanjaro

Ameg Lodge sets itself apart from many other hotels with its fitness center. Image source: ameglodge.com

Constructed with eco-friendly materials, Ameg Lodge Kilimanjaro blends traditional and modern African styles. The hotel is located in the Shanty Town area, near Brubru Lodge, one of our top picks. This area is considered an ideal base for travelers before and after their Kilimanjaro trek.

Ameg Lodge Kilimanjaro distinguishes itself with its excellent facilities and high level of service. Image source: ameglodge.com

The hotel features guest rooms, a restaurant serving continental and international cuisine, a pool with a relaxation area, and a fitness center. It also offers airport transfers and hotel shuttle bus for an additional fee.

Website: ameglodge.com

The Maridadi Hotel

The rooms at Maridadi offer a view of Mount Kilimanjaro. Image source: themaridadihotel.com

The newly opened Maridadi Hotel, launched in 2024, is just 36 km (22 miles) from Kilimanjaro. Its rooms offer panoramic views of the summit.  

Guests have access to a swimming pool, a shared lounge area, a restaurant serving African, American, and Chinese cuisine, and a terrace with a bar. Additionally, the hotel provides free Wi-Fi, parking, and the option to book a paid airport transfer.

The hotel has everything needed for a complete and relaxing stay. Image source: themaridadihotel.com

Website: themaridadihotel.com

This concludes our list of the top hotels offering travelers exceptional comfort and high-quality service. However, we also recommend considering a few more hotels that have received consistently high or relatively high ratings:

Kibo Palace Hotel

The hotel features a rooftop terrace with a view of Kilimanjaro. Image source: kibopalacehotel.com

Located in the heart of Moshi, this city-style hotel may not have a garden or swimming pool, but it makes up for it with stunning views of Kilimanjaro, which guests can enjoy from their rooms or the open-air rooftop bar.

The restaurant serves African, American, and Chinese cuisine. In reviews on booking platforms, travelers highlight the good service and delicious food. However, some visitors mention the high cost of stay and credit card payment fees.

Website: kibopalacehotel.com

Parkview Inn

Parkview Inn is convenient for those who want to explore the streets of Moshi. Image source: pvim.com

The hotel is located in the town center, within walking distance of key attractions, making it ideal for both exploring the city and resting before or after the Kilimanjaro trek. Guests can relax by the outdoor pool in the peaceful inner courtyard or enjoy the rooftop terrace. The on-site restaurant offers a mix of international and Indian cuisine.

However, on booking platforms, some Parkview Inn visitors have complained about poor service and the condition of the rooms.

Website: pvim.com

Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel

Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel is suitable for business travelers and family vacations. It features a swimming pool, a children's playground, and a conference hall. Image source: kiliwonders.com

Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel markets itself as one of Moshi's first 4-star boutique hotels. It is located 4 km (2.5 miles) from the city and 40 km (25 miles) from the airport. The hotel features 44 rooms, a rooftop bar with views of Mt Kilimanjaro, and two restaurants offering international cuisine, barbecue, and live music.

Among guests who rated their stay at Kilimanjaro Wonders Hotel as “Good,” common complaints included inconsistent service, night power outages, and unreliable Wi-Fi.

Website: kiliwonders.com

Kilimanjaro White House Hotel

View of Mount Kilimanjaro from the hotel. Image source: booking.com

Kilimanjaro White House Hotel is one of the budget hotels located approximately 4 km (2.5 miles) from central Moshi. The hotel offers 23 comfortable rooms, some of which provide views of Mount Kilimanjaro. On-site amenities include a restaurant and bar serving a variety of dishes, and free Wi-Fi access.

Guests have noted the cleanliness of the rooms and the friendly, helpful staff. However, some reviews mention challenges such as inconsistent Wi-Fi connectivity and the hotel's distance from the city center.

Website: https://www.kilimanjaro-whitehousehotel.com/

Panone Hotels - King'ori Kilimanjaro Airport

The hotel's convenient location near Kilimanjaro Airport makes it ideal for layovers. Image source: booking.com

Panone Hotels - King'ori Kilimanjaro Airport is a 4-star hotel just 15 km from the airport, making it a convenient choice for travelers. The hotel features 40 rooms, each with a private bathroom, flat-screen TV, and free Wi-Fi. Guests can also use a 24-hour front desk, room service, and currency exchange.

Many guests praise the staff for being efficient and friendly. However, some mention that while the rooms are simple, they are adequate for a short stay.

Website: https://panonehotels.co.tz/

Dual Mountain View Lodge

The lodge is surrounded by banana plantations and overlooks a lush coffee farm. Image source: booking.com

Dual Mountain View Lodge is a peaceful getaway in Mweka village, about 9 miles (14.5 km) from Moshi Town and close to Mweka Gate. The lodge features 15 rooms, each with a private bathroom and comfy bed. Guests can dine at the rooftop restaurant and bar while enjoying stunning views of Mount Meru and Kilimanjaro. Other amenities include a well-maintained swimming pool, free WiFi, airport transfers, and complimentary parking.

Guest reviews highlight the views and the friendly, helpful staff but mention occasional Wi-Fi disruptions and plumbing issues.

Website: https://dualmountainviewlodge.com/

Top 5 hostels in Moshi

For budget-conscious travelers, Moshi offers several great hostel options. Based on traveler reviews, here are some of the best picks:

Villa Rosa

Villa Rosa stands out for its cozy lounge area, warm hospitality, and clean rooms

The hostel is located 40 km (25 miles) from Mt Kilimanjaro. It offers bicycles and parking, a shared lounge in the garden, a restaurant, a bar, and free Wi-Fi. Travelers praise the good service, food, and room maintenance.

Mama Thea homes

Travelers highlight the good service and beautiful green garden at Mama Thea Homes

It's more of a boutique guesthouse than a traditional hotel. It offers comfortable rooms, a lush green garden, a relaxation area, free bicycles, and Wi-Fi. Some rooms even have a fully equipped kitchen, including a refrigerator. The restaurant serves continental breakfasts and African, American, and Chinese cuisine.

Hostel Kiliwood

Hostel Kiliwood is among the few hostels that provide evening entertainment for guests

Kiliwood is 43 km (27 miles) from Kilimanjaro National Park and 4 km (2.5 miles) from Moshi’s historic railway station. The hostel features a shared lounge with evening entertainment, a garden, and free parking. Wi-Fi and breakfast are included in the stay. Guests can also rent bicycles and cars on-site.

Kiwavi Home

Kiwavi Home has one of the highest ratings from travelers

Kiwavi Home has earned high praise from travelers. Located just 2.5 km (1.5 miles) from the central Moshi and 42 km (26 miles) from the National Park, the guesthouse offers family rooms, a garden, a shared lounge, a terrace, and a barbecue area. Guests can enjoy free breakfast in a communal kitchen, free WiFi, and on-site parking. Each room has a safe, and bicycle or car rentals are available. An airport transfer service can be arranged for an additional fee.

Kilimanjaro Trekcity Hostel

According to traveler reviews, Trekcity Hostel stands out for offering some of the best service in the budget category

Trekcity Hostel provides a range of accommodation options, from shared male and female dorms to private rooms, including a deluxe option with a spacious double bed. Guests can enjoy free bicycle rentals, Wi-Fi, parking, a garden, a shared lounge, a bar, and an on-site restaurant.
