No Hunting
We don’t support hunting or animal exploitation
In Tanzania, a large part of the tourism industry is directly or indirectly linked to hunting. Many lodges, aviation companies, and even safari operators are owned by hunting groups or work closely with them. We do not sell, promote, or collaborate with any companies, lodges, or services associated with the hunting industry or with activities that exploit wildlife for entertainment. We believe that wild animals must remain wild, free, and unharmed, observed only in their natural habitat. Our safaris are built on respect for wildlife and on partnerships that align with responsible, non-consumptive tourism. This principle guides every decision we make and every experience we create.

5-Seat Extended-Space

Vehicles & Comfort
Our Signature “Angelino Style” Layout
Most of our safari vehicles are configured as 5-seaters: four seats in the back and one next to the driver. In the same long-base Land Cruisers where almost all operators install six seats, we intentionally keep only four passenger seats in the rear. This gives you much more legroom, easier movement inside the vehicle, and clear, unobstructed viewing angles for wildlife observation. We introduced this configuration in 2015, and it has become so well known for its comfort and premium feel that even manufacturers now refer to this layout as the “Angelino style”.

We Never Drive
Off-Road
Vehicles & Comfort
Protecting Wildlife and Fragile Ecosystems
We strictly follow national park rules and never drive off-road. Leaving marked routes accelerates soil erosion, crushes fragile plants, destroys bird nests, harms insects, and can even kill hidden young animals like serval kittens. Staying on roads helps protect Tanzania’s ecosystems for the long term.

Your safari should respect the landscapes you explore, and with Angelino, it always does.

Flying Doctors
Safety
Fast Emergency Air Support
All Angelino safari guests are automatically covered by Flying Doctors, which provides rapid air evacuation in case of a medical emergency. Unlike standard travel insurance, which may take hours to authorize assistance, Flying Doctors can respond within minutes. When time is critical, this speed can be life-saving.
Although Angelino has had no evacuations in 11 years, incidents occur on safaris across Tanzania each month, often due to unsafe driving practices or a lack of seatbelt use. Your safety is our highest priority, and Flying Doctors coverage gives you an additional layer of protection throughout your journey.

What is a B Corp?
Certified B Corps are for-profit companies independently vetted by the non-profit B Lab. They must meet rigorous standards in:
  • Social & environmental performance
  • Operational transparency
  • Accountability to all stakeholders (not just shareholders)
To earn the certification, a company must score 80+ on the B Impact Assessment, make a legal commitment to stakeholder governance, and publish its full impact profile publicly.
Why it matters:
  • As a certified B Corp, Angelino is legally required to consider the impact of its decisions on people, communities, and the environment — not just profit. We’re committed to ethical tourism, local empowerment, and environmental responsibility. We do not partner with companies that promote hunting, animal exploitation, or other ethically questionable activities. Our compliance and impact are verified through independent audits every three years.
Results and support:
  • For you, this means verified standards you can trust, travel that supports local jobs and communities, and a company committed to long-term responsibility.
  • Supporters of the B Corp include Leonardo DiCaprio, Angelina Jolie, former U.S. Presidents Al Gore and Bill Clinton, Nobel Prize–winning economist Robert Shiller, and business leaders like Richard Branson of Virgin and Paul Polman of Unilever.

Perfected Safari Itineraries
Experience & Conclusion
We Design Safaris That Are Never Rushed
With 11 years of experience in Tanzania and thousands of safaris under our belt, we’ve learned how to design routes that offer the highest chance of great wildlife sightings without unnecessary transfers, long drives, or daily repacking. Our itineraries focus on smart park sequencing, efficient logistics, and lodge locations chosen for both comfort and proximity to key wildlife areas.
Think of your safari as a well-paced holiday: start with an early morning game drive, return to the lodge for lunch and a break during the hottest hours, then head out again in the afternoon when animals are active.

Staying three to four nights in one carefully selected lodge is both comfortable and restorative, and it often increases your chances of exceptional wildlife sightings.
