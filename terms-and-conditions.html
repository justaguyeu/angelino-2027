These restrictions are implemented solely in the interest of guest safety and risk prevention and apply regardless of prior climbing experience, physical fitness, accompaniment by family members, or the presence of private medical personnel.

Angelino Adventures maintains one of the strongest safety records on Mount Kilimanjaro, with a zero-fatality incident record across all guided expeditions.
